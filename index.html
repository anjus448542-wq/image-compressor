Understanding Image Formats & Compression

JPEG / JPG Compression

JPEG uses lossy compression by discarding fine details that human eyes hardly notice. It is best suited for photographs, complex scenery, and continuous tone images. You can adjust the quality slider to balance visual clarity against file weight.

PNG Optimization

PNG is a lossless format ideal for graphics with sharp edges, logos, and transparent backgrounds. Standard PNGs can be heavy; SmartCompress quantizes color palettes to significantly reduce PNG weight while retaining transparency.

WebP Compression

Developed by Google, WebP offers superior lossy and lossless compression. WebP images are typically 25% to 35% smaller than equivalent JPEG images at identical visual quality. Converting legacy JPGs or PNGs to WebP is a great strategy to improve website loading speeds.

Why does image quality drop when compressing under 20 KB?
